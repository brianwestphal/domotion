DM-1790 — validating that the asymmetric harness mode actually discriminates
===========================================================================

Method: run the same 11 `02-text` html-test fixtures under all three browser
configurations and compare the aggregate coverage. A mode that cannot produce
three different numbers is not an instrument.

macOS — all three identical, and that is the TRUTH, not a bug
------------------------------------------------------------
  default     11 fixtures  mean 0.0067%  max 0.0272%  6 passing
  symmetric   11 fixtures  mean 0.0067%  max 0.0272%  6 passing
  asymmetric  11 fixtures  mean 0.0067%  max 0.0272%  6 passing

Probe (one fixture, 1024x768, sha256 of the screenshot bytes):
  (none)                       e1d105828fa3de81
  --font-render-hinting=none   e1d105828fa3de81
  --disable-lcd-text           e1d105828fa3de81

Byte-identical. CoreText ignores the FreeType hinting flag, and Playwright's
headless Chromium already paints without LCD subpixel text. A flag that changes
nothing cannot produce three different numbers.

(An earlier run of this same table was invalid: the expected-PNG cache key did
not include the capture flags, so the flagged runs silently reused the unflagged
cached screenshots. Fixed by `captureFlagsCacheToken()`; the numbers above are
from after the fix, and the cache was observed to split — 27 -> 38 entries when
a new capture-flag condition was introduced.)

Linux — the pinned Playwright container, where --disable-lcd-text DOES paint differently
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Probe on the same fixture:
  (none)                       fa2a571a050c082d
  --font-render-hinting=none   fa2a571a050c082d      <- also a no-op here, for this fixture
  --disable-lcd-text           29f192fca608b99e      <- differs

  default (no flags)                       mean 0.0157%  max 0.0524%  5/11 passing
  symmetric (--disable-lcd-text on both)   mean 0.0183%  max 0.0590%  3/11 passing
  asymmetric (capture flagged, raster not) mean 0.0164%  max 0.0670%  8/11 passing

Three genuinely different results. The load-bearing observation is that the
asymmetric number is NOT between the other two: coupling the two sides does not
yield a conservative estimate of the consumer's condition, it yields a number
that is not about the consumer's condition at all.

Not a recommendation: on this subset, capturing with --disable-lcd-text while
the consumer rasterizes with LCD text on scored better than the fully-default
run. One flag, 11 fixtures, one platform.

Also worth recording: --font-render-hinting=none was a no-op on this container
for this fixture, so DM-1789's paths-mode result came from the feature suite's
fonts, not from these.

Run via: HTML_TEST_OUTPUT_DIR=... CMD="..." bash scripts/test-linux-docker.sh
(redirected output dir because the expected-PNG cache is not platform-keyed —
a pre-existing hazard, noted in docs/105 "Not covered").
