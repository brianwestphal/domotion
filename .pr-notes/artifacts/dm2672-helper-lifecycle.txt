Interpretation
  Persistent transport is the production-faithful lifecycle and removes about
  74 ms of helper process overhead in this controlled profile, but it is not the
  dominant runtime cost. Text rendering accounts for 4.79 s and the remaining
  capture/serialization path for about 7.8 s. A qemu-emulated x64 run that took
  more than two minutes was rejected as native-CI performance evidence.

Source-lifetime audit
  Chromium 7d859f271cbda744098ac69f44978d4edfa62be3:
    Blink FontCache returns process-owned cached platform/font data, and
    HarfBuzzFontCache::GetOrCreate memoizes shaped font state by unique id.
  Skia ebf50520d720a1ce9d842d942d04c6c39c3fbc7b:
    SkFontMgr_FCI owns request/typeface caches; SkTypeface_FreeType initializes
    its FaceRec once. Reusing an opened face for same-key requests matches that
    source lifecycle; per-request helper spawning is a diagnostic transport,
    not Chromium/Skia behavior.
